Support

Buzzkill uses Apple Shortcuts and Color Filters to guide a one-time local setup. If something is not working, the setup checklist and issue tracker are the best places to start.

Open a support issue Read the setup guide

When reporting a problem

Please include your iOS version, the step that failed, and what you expected to happen. Never include passwords, private keys, device identifiers, screenshots with personal information, or other sensitive data.

Buzzkill does not require an account or sign-in.
