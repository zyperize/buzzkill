What Buzzkill does

Buzzkill guides you through a one-time local setup using Apple Shortcuts and the iPhone’s Accessibility Color Filters. It installs two bundled shortcuts and explains how to create the matching Personal Automations yourself.

Buzzkill does not block apps, impose time limits, monitor your usage, or inspect content. It cannot change your Color Filters setting directly. The automations run in Apple Shortcuts, under your control, and you can delete them at any time.
