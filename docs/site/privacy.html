Buzzkill for iPhone

Privacy policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026.

Summary

Buzzkill does not collect, transmit, store on a remote server, or share personal data. Everything you configure stays on your device.

What Buzzkill stores

Buzzkill stores local setup state, such as whether a bundled shortcut installer was opened, whether the local shortcut test passed, and whether setup was marked complete. These values use the operating system’s local app storage and are not uploaded.

What Buzzkill reads

On iOS, Buzzkill reads whether the system grayscale setting is enabled so it can show setup status. It cannot change that protected setting directly.

What Buzzkill does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party identifiers.
  • No background uploads, location, microphone, camera, or contacts access.
  • No app blocking, shielding, time limits, usage monitoring, or content inspection.

Questions

For privacy questions, please use the public Buzzkill support issues. Please do not include private data in a report.
